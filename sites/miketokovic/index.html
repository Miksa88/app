01Mobile · Web · White-label

fitbyivana

An autonomous training & nutrition coach.

A coach in your pocket that adapts every week. It plans the training and the food, then adjusts as life actually goes.

  • 8-layer adaptive programming engine. Mesocycles, effort-based autoregulation, automatic exercise swaps for injuries, smart calorie adjustments, refeeds and diet breaks.
  • iOS app and web. One codebase, shipped with Capacitor.
  • Bilingual, two themes. Serbian and English, dark and light.
  • White-label by design. Each trainer gets the app under their own brand from a single config.
  • Calm by design. Anti-anxiety UX — no streaks, no guilt for missed days.

How a project flows

Five steps, one direction. Each hands a clear result to the next.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Goals, users, constraints. We agree what to build first, and what to leave out.

  2. 02

    Design

    Flows, interfaces and a working prototype you can react to before code is written.

  3. 03

    Build

    Small, tested increments. Type-checked, with unit and end-to-end tests behind them.

  4. 04

    Ship

    Deployment, App Store submission and the launch checklist, handled.

  5. 05

    Evolve

    Maintenance, iteration and new features — the work that keeps a product running.
