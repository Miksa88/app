Privacy notice

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This notice explains what happens to your information when you visit miketokovic.com, the website of Tokovic LLC. The short version: we do not ask for your data, and we do not track you.

What this site does not do

  • It has no forms and does not collect information you type into the page.
  • It sets no cookies.
  • It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is served by Cloudflare. To deliver the site and keep it secure, Cloudflare may process technical information about each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We do not use this information to identify you.

Fonts

The site’s typefaces, Geist and Geist Mono, are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and standard request details, as with any file loaded from a third party.

Email

If you write to office@miketokovic.com, we use what you send only to reply to you. If you would like an email thread deleted, ask and we will delete it.

Changes

If this notice changes, we will update the effective date above.
